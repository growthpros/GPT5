# Competitor landscape: online word search generators and makers (checked 13 Nov 2026)

Method note: prices and features below were read from the vendors' own pages on 13 Nov 2026 unless marked otherwise. Several pages blocked automated fetching (Canva, EduPics, Semrush overview) or were not reached (Twinkl, Education.com pricing, MagicSchool/Diffit/Brisk tool lists, ProProfs, TheWordSearch maker detail); those items are flagged as search-snippet level or listed under Gaps. Non-USD prices are given in the currency shown on the page.

## Who are the main players?

### Takeaway
The market splits into five groups: (1) free legacy generators funded by ads or a parent brand (Discovery Puzzlemaker, TheWordSearch.com, Teacher's Corner, Super Teacher Worksheets), (2) low-priced paywalled teacher tools at about $5 to $10 a month (BrightSprout/My Word Search, WordMint, Wordwall, Puzzel.org, Teach Starter), (3) the free, ad-free "Labs" family with an optional small membership (Word Search Labs, Crossword Labs), (4) publisher-grade embeddable platforms at $99+ a month (Amuse Labs PuzzleMe), and (5) a fast-growing long tail of free AI theme-to-puzzle tools (Template.net, Supercoloring, KDPBuilder, Canva apps, EduPics, Teacher's Corner). Most are print-first and teacher-first; a consumer "type a theme and play it on screen" product is not the core proposition of any of the big names.

### Inferences

- The strongest free incumbents (Word Search Labs, Super Teacher Worksheets, Puzzlemaker) give away create, print, answer key and, for Labs, online play and sharing. A new entrant cannot charge for those basics.

## Which competitors already generate the word list from a typed theme using AI?

### Takeaway
AI theme-to-word-list is already common and mostly free, but it lives in the long tail and in general platforms rather than in the biggest legacy word search brands. None of the incumbents with the most traffic (Puzzlemaker, WordMint, BrightSprout, Word Search Labs, Super Teacher Worksheets) advertise it on the pages checked. The feature alone is not a moat.

### Cited Findings
- Free, no sign-up, theme in and puzzle out: Template.net (text or voice prompt, 20+ languages). — [Template.net](https://www.template.net/ai-word-search-generator)
- Free with control over topic, difficulty and word count, with the ability to remove suggestions: Supercoloring. — [Supercoloring](https://www.supercoloring.com/tool/word-search-generator)
- Free theme-to-grid with answer key as a lead magnet for a paid book studio: KDPBuilder. — [KDPBuilder](https://kdpbuilder.com/free-tools/word-search-generator)
- "A.I. list" as one of three word-list sources inside an established ad-supported teacher tool that also offers online play and sharing: The Teacher's Corner. — [Teacher's Corner](https://worksheets.theteacherscorner.net/make-your-own/word-search/)
- AI prompts or source text to puzzle inside Canva, free on any plan: Canva Word Search Maker app. — [Canva app listing](https://www.canva.com/apps/AAG83en-zjY/word-search-maker)
- AI metered by credits: Puzzel.org gives 3 one-time credits free, 5 to 25 a month on paid plans, €0.50 per extra generation. — [Puzzel.org pricing](https://puzzel.org/en/pricing)
- AI gated behind payment: Wordwall "Generate With AI" is Standard/Pro only, and the Wordsearch template itself is Pro only ($10.80/month). — [Wordwall price plans](https://wordwall.net/price-plans)
- AI included in all plans for publishers: PuzzleMe AI. — [Amuse Labs pricing](https://amuselabs.com/pricing)
- No AI mentioned on the pages checked for Puzzlemaker, WordMint, BrightSprout, Word Search Labs, Super Teacher Worksheets or the Teach Starter widget. — [Puzzlemaker](https://puzzlemaker.discoveryeducation.com/word-search); [WordMint](https://wordmint.com/pricing); [BrightSprout](https://brightsprout.com/word-search-maker); [Word Search Labs](https://wordsearchlabs.com/); [STW](https://www.superteacherworksheets.com/generator-word-search.html); [Teach Starter](https://www.teachstarter.com/us/widget/word-search-us/)

### Inferences
- The combination that looks least served is: AI theme input, instant on-screen play on mobile, no login, and a share link. Teacher's Corner comes closest (AI list plus playable/shareable) but is ad-heavy and teacher-framed; Puzzel.org is close but caps free play at 60 sessions a month and 3 AI credits.
- Because free AI word lists are already available, the paid value has to sit elsewhere: quality and age-appropriateness of the list, play experience, saved collections, classroom assignment and results, branding, bulk or book export, or commercial rights.

### Gaps
- Quality of AI word lists was not hands-on tested for any tool; "how good" is unanswered beyond feature descriptions.
- MagicSchool, Diffit and Brisk: searches found no evidence of a dedicated word search tool in any of the three, but their own tool lists were not fetched, so absence is not confirmed. MagicSchool Plus pricing was reported by a third-party review as $8.33/user/month annual or $12.99 monthly (checked May 2026) via [kuraplan review](https://kuraplan.com/reviews/magicschool-ai-review); a conflicting figure appeared elsewhere. Treat as unverified.
- Whether Canva's app produces an answer key or interactive play was not confirmed.
- General chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) can produce word lists and simple grids on request; no source was gathered on how much teacher usage this displaces.

## What do users complain about or praise, and where are the gaps?

### Takeaway
Direct user-voice evidence (Reddit, teacher forums, Common Sense reviews) was not retrieved, so this section rests mainly on product-page gating and competitor commentary. The visible friction points are paywalled PDF export and printing, ad dependence, browser-bound saving, print-first design with weak or absent online play, and no visible accessibility features on any page checked.

### Cited Findings
- Paywalled basics: WordMint blocks PDF and Word export on the free tier; BrightSprout blocks printing and online solving entirely until payment. — [WordMint pricing](https://wordmint.com/pricing); [BrightSprout](https://brightsprout.com/word-search-maker)
- Ad dependence and ad-blocker penalties: Teacher's Corner serves a reduced version to users with ad blockers and sells premium to remove ads; free saves are tied to browser cookies and are lost if cookies are cleared. — [Teacher's Corner](https://worksheets.theteacherscorner.net/make-your-own/word-search/)
- Quality-control burden left to the user: Puzzlemaker and Super Teacher Worksheets both warn users to proofread because random fill can form unintended words; STW may silently drop words that do not fit. — [Puzzlemaker](https://puzzlemaker.discoveryeducation.com/word-search); [STW](https://www.superteacherworksheets.com/generator-word-search.html)
- Print-only limits: Flippity says its word searches are designed to be printed, not played online; Puzzlemaker outputs only image or HTML. — [Flippity](https://www.flippity.net/v2/WordSearch.htm); [Puzzlemaker](https://puzzlemaker.discoveryeducation.com/word-search)
- Curt support posture at Word Search Labs: FAQ answers include "Tough cookies. What you see is what you get." on embed customization and advice to get "a better browser or operating system" for PDF printing. — [Word Search Labs about](https://wordsearchlabs.com/about)
- What vendors treat as selling points (a proxy for what users value): no ads, no watermarks, no registration (Word Search Labs, Crossword Labs); instant PDF with solution page and no account (edu-games.org, STW). — [Word Search Labs](https://wordsearchlabs.com/); [Crossword Labs about](https://crosswordlabs.com/about); [edu-games.org](https://www.edu-games.org/word-games/word-search-maker)
- One roundup characterizes most free printable puzzle sites as "thin content wrapped around ads, five puzzles deep and then a paywall". Low-authority blog. — [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/where-to-find-free-printable-puzzle-games/)
- Roundup articles ranking these tools disagree with each other and include affiliate or paid placements; they are weak evidence of user sentiment. — [Gitnux](https://gitnux.org/best/word-search-maker-software/); [WifiTalents](https://wifitalents.com/best/word-search-maker-software/)
- None of the product pages fetched mention large print, dyslexia-friendly fonts, screen-reader support or colour-contrast options. Teach Starter offers "school-style" fonts and Teacher's Corner offers font and colour choices, which is the closest found. — [Teach Starter widget](https://www.teachstarter.com/us/widget/word-search-us/); [Teacher's Corner](https://worksheets.theteacherscorner.net/make-your-own/word-search/)

### Inferences
- Accessibility (large print, dyslexia-friendly fonts, high contrast, touch-friendly selection) is unclaimed positioning among the tools reviewed. This is an absence-of-evidence finding from marketing pages, not a tested one.
- A no-login, ad-free, mobile-playable flow with AI theme input competes directly with Word Search Labs on the "clean and free" axis; Labs' lack of AI and its print/desktop heritage are the openings.
- Safety filtering of AI word lists and of accidental words in the fill is a real differentiator for a school audience, given that incumbents push proofreading onto the teacher.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/Teachers, r/puzzles), teacher-forum or Common Sense Education review content was retrieved; the targeted search returned none. User praise and complaints in users' own words remain unverified.
- Mobile play quality of each tool was not tested.
- No Trustpilot, G2 or app-store ratings were gathered.
