- **VERIFIED (search-derived):** at least one primary listing (show feed, official site, or official directory) agrees.

## Q1: What exists, who runs it, and is it active in 2026?

### Takeaway
There is a small but real ecosystem. A few explicitly spiritual ND podcasts are active in 2025–2026: The NeuroDivine (Sufi/ADHD), AuDHD Magic (witchy/intuitive), and several Christian shows. SquarePeg (a general late-diagnosed ND podcast) is still releasing weekly, with Season 11 in 2026. On the program side, Catholic and Anglican ministries are active, and the Church of England formally debated neurodiversity in 2026. Buddhist ND sanghas (Plum Village) are active online. Retreats are rare and one-off. The one ND meditation retreat confirmed for 2026 (Neurospicy, Boone NC, July 16–19) has already happened, and no 2027 date was found.

## Q2: What gaps remain (especially for late-diagnosed AuDHD women in midlife)?

### Takeaway
No program, retreat, podcast or community was found that combines all three of AuDHD, late diagnosis or midlife women, and spirituality. Midlife ND women's groups (AANE 50+, ADDA 50+, Aligned + Alive) are secular and split by diagnosis. ND spirituality offerings either address everyone who is ND, are Christian-institutional, or are small solo-host esoteric podcasts.

### Cited Findings
- Groups for women 50+ are diagnosis-specific and secular: AANE's is for autistic women 50+, and ADDA's is for ADHD women over 50. Neither mentions spirituality. — [AANE](https://aane.org/events/support-group-for-women-50-4-27-26-630-pm-800-pm-et/); [ADDA](https://add.org/virtual-peer-support-group-women-50/)

### Inferences
- **Gap 1, intersection:** nothing found is designed for late-diagnosed AuDHD women in midlife, perimenopause or menopause seeking spiritual or contemplative support.
- **Gap 2, continuity:** ND-spirituality podcasts tend to be short-lived or sporadic (NeuroDivine: 4 episodes over about 4 years). Retreats are one-offs. Recurring options are mainly Buddhist sanghas and secular peer groups.
- **Gap 3, non-Christian and non-institutional paths:** pagan and witchy AuDHD content exists only as small podcasts (AuDHD Magic, Unshaming AuDHD). No Jewish or adult Muslim ND spiritual communities were found, and the Sufi presence is one podcast.
- **Gap 4, trained guides:** there is very little training for spiritual directors on ND. One webinar and one programme mention were found.
- **Gap 5, Australia:** Australian offerings found are therapeutic retreats with mindfulness elements, not spiritual communities. 2026 dates were unconfirmed.
- **Gap 6, accessible retreat formats:** Autsit's tiny-retreat model (4–5 people) and Neurospicy's built-in free time point to sensory-aware design, but no women's or AuDHD version was found.

- Searches were US-indexed and snippet-based. UK and Australian Facebook groups, Discord servers, Patreon communities and Substack-only offerings are probably under-counted.
