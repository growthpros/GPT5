HR Due Diligence Readiness

What a buyer is likely to ask HR for, ranked, and where Copilot can help

Prepared for Erin Hughes, Ph.D., The Haverford Trust Company, by Caroline Hinton, Growth Partner Pros. November 2026.

Why this list

Erin, here's the stack-ranked list I promised. If a transaction moves forward, the buyer's team will send HR a long request list, usually with short deadlines and in a format of their choosing. This puts that list in order, so you can see what's almost certain to come and what can wait.

I built it for a wealth management and trust firm of about 150 people with a small HR team, using published buyer request lists, banking and securities rules and recent deal commentary. The ranking itself is my judgment.

This is a readiness guide, not legal advice. Your deal counsel and the buyer's actual request list come first. Anything that touches agreements, classification, benefits or pay should go past counsel before you change it.

Where I'd start

You don't need to do all of this now. These three steps cover the requests most likely to come first, and each one makes the rest easier:

1
One restricted place for diligence filesA SharePoint library with access limited to named people. Move files into it rather than copying them, so there's one version of everything.
2
The employee censusOne reconciled spreadsheet of everyone. Almost every other request leans on it.
3
Every signed agreement in one folderOffer letters, employment agreements and restrictive covenants. For an advisory firm, this is often the request most likely to affect price.

The ranked list

What a buyer is likely to ask for

Likelihood. Near certain: on almost every buyer's first request list. Likely: most buyers of a wealth and trust firm will ask, usually in the first or second round. Possible: depends on the buyer and the deal structure.

Effort, assuming the documents exist and someone has to find, check and organize them. Light: a few hours to a day. Medium: two to five working days. Heavy: one to three weeks of on-and-off work. If something has to be created from scratch, plan for about double.

Where AI helps

Five places Copilot can
carry real weight

Copilot is good with words: finding, summarizing and drafting. Excel, SharePoint and people handle the math, the permissions and the final call.
