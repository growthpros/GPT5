Copilot uses the access people already have

Most AI tools only know what you paste into them. Microsoft 365 Copilot works inside email, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint, and it draws on what the person using it is allowed to open. Microsoft's own guidance says that if content is already overshared, Copilot can surface it to anyone who has access [1].

That's why I start with the work and the information it touches, not with the tool. For every Copilot use, I ask six questions, in this order.

1
OwnerWho owns the outcome? One named person, with a title.
2
DataWhat does Copilot actually need to know for this task, and nothing more?
3
EnvironmentWhere is it processed, and who else touches it?
4
AccessWhat can Copilot reach?
5
ActionWhat can Copilot change, and does a person approve it first?
6
OversightWhere must a human decide, and how do we check what comes out?
It will feel familiar

These mirror controls a finance team already runs for money: a budget owner, need to know, approved vendors, system access, approval limits and review after the fact.

What stays human

Hiring, pay, promotion, reviews and anything a regulator could ask about stay with people. Copilot drafts and a person decides. For the 360 summaries, HR reads every summary before a manager sees it.

Worth checking now

Three things to look at
before HR files move

Each one is a short conversation with whoever manages your Microsoft 365 settings.

1. Whether Claude is switched on

Some Copilot features can use Claude, Anthropic's model. It's on by default for most commercial tenants outside the EU, EFTA and UK, and an admin can turn it off or limit it to chosen groups. Models labeled "Anthropic models with Data Retention" run under Anthropic's own terms, so compliance should review those before anyone uses them [2].

2. Who can already open what

Copilot can surface any file a person has access to, including files shared more widely than anyone meant. Microsoft provides reports that show broadly shared sites [3], and a setting that keeps a site out of Copilot while permissions are cleaned up [4]. Setting access before files move into SharePoint is much easier than fixing it after.

3. Whether Copilot activity is being kept

Purview can keep Copilot prompts and responses for as long as your records rules require, but Copilot has its own retention location, so existing policies don't cover it until someone adds it [5]. Audit records are kept 180 days by default [6].
